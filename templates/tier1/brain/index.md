# Brain index: the recall map
<!-- Format: one table, Topic | File | Cap | Read full when. Cap: 60 lines. Add a row when a new file appears. -->

All paths are under ~/cogent. At the start of every session a SLIM load shows me: soul.md, the newer of the newest handoff and now.md (by the time in the handoff's filename and now.md's `updated:` line; a tie goes to the handoff), the top 8 entries of failed.md, the NEVER lines of gotchas.md, rules.md and the HIGH claims. A FULL load (every brain file except archive/) runs when she says "load everything" and at the first session of each week. The rest I open only when the task matches.

| Topic | File | Cap | Read full when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who I am and who I work for | soul.md | 6 KB | Always shown. Only she changes it. |
| Where we left off | brain/now.md | 40 lines | Starts with an `updated:` time. Shown unless a newer or equal-time handoff exists. "Continue" means read the newer of the two. |
| Unfinished work | brain/handoff/YYYY-MM-DD-HHMM-topic.md | 40 lines each | The newest one is shown. Older than 30 days is archived. |
| Never again | brain/failed.md | 8 KB | Top 8 shown. Open it all before a risky or repeated task. |
| Traps in her work | brain/gotchas.md | 8 KB | NEVER lines shown. Open it all before a task that touches a known trap. |
| Standing rules | brain/rules.md | 40 rules | Always shown. Open it to check a rule's reason. |
| What I believe, with confidence | brain/claims.md | 60 claims | HIGH claims shown. Open it when she asks me something and I need the confidence. |
| Ideas to try | brain/ideas.md | 30 lines | When choosing what to do next, or at wrap-up. |
| Raw lessons, not yet proven | brain/journal/YYYY-MM.md | none | When a lesson looks familiar, or at wrap-up to see if one was seen twice. |
| Write ledger | brain/log.md | 100 lines | When she asks "when did you learn that?" |
| Older material | brain/archive/ | none | Rarely. Search it with grep when something is not in the files above. |
