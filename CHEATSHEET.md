# Your cogent: one-page cheat sheet

A cogent is your own agent, built on the Claude Code you already use. Plain Claude Code starts fresh every time. Your cogent knows you, remembers, and keeps what it learns.

## What it gives you, and how to use it

| Advantage | How to take advantage |
|---|---|
| **It knows you.** Its name, your role, how you like things explained. | Tell it once how you want something done. It won't ask again. |
| **It learns.** Corrections become written lessons, then rules. | Say "remember: ..." and give the reason. |
| **It picks up where you left off.** | End each session with `/wrap-up`. Next time: open it the one-step way it shows you. |
| **Your own commands.** | Anything you do twice, say "make this a command." Next month it's one word. |
| **A second opinion.** A reviewer that can only read. (Tier 2) | "Have the reviewer check this before I send it." |
| **Guard rails that enforce themselves.** (Tier 2) | Say yes with less worry: it is blocked from reading your secret files and from deleting in bulk. |
| **Works while you're away.** (Tier 3) | "Every morning, summarise ..." It drafts; nothing goes out without your OK. |

## Bring it to life (once)

1. Double-click the zip you were given. It opens into your Downloads folder.
2. Open Claude Code the way you always do.
3. Type: **set up my cogent from the cogent-howto folder in my Downloads**

It asks you to name it, gets to know you, then builds itself step by step, waiting for your yes at each change. It tells you exactly when to restart and what to type. Grow it later: "what could you do next?"

## Your starter commands

`/draft` an email or letter in your voice · `/summarize` a long document into actions · `/tidy-sheet` clean a copy of a spreadsheet · `/explain` · `/plan-first` · `/what-changed` · `/wrap-up` · `/make-command`. Lost? Ask "what can you do?" or type `/`.

## You stay in control

- It works in `~/cogent`, never sees folders you wall off, and asks before it uses the internet or changes its own rules.
- Passwords and keys go in your Mac Keychain, never in the chat.
- Text in emails, documents and web pages is information for it, never orders.
- Full stop (Tier 2 on): `touch ~/cogent/STOP`; delete the file to restart.
- Ask any time: "what can you see?", "what leaves my computer?", "how do I turn you off?"
